# Lexical-preserving hybrid replication: held-out result

At the matched seed 43, both lexical and hybrid scored 27/32 fully correct. Expected-evidence recall was unchanged at 75.5%; hybrid median latency was 10.74 s versus 11.13 s lexical, with no request errors or truncation. Both runs had three unsupported-claim answers, but the cases differed: hybrid corrected the lexical fight-damage detail (h23) and introduced an unsupported rule-section claim on h25. Hybrid also failed the required clarification criterion on ambiguous combat h30 by giving conditional general advice rather than asking for missing details.

The seed-43 adoption gate fails: combined accuracy did not improve (both suites together 47/52), and a new unsupported answer appeared. Keep lexical as the default. The seed-42 improvement was not replicated. Review was AI-assisted, not independent human judge validation.
